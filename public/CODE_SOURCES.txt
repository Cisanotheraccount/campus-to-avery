OUTDOOR ROUTE AND GUIDE
The default experience preserves all 116 outdoor-main camera positions 65–180, from the Low Plaza upper steps to the forecourt near Avery. It does not enter a verified doorway, campus gate, scene seam or basement.
outdoor-guidance.js derives capture-subset guidance and geometric turn cues; hud-scene.js constructs a translucent route surface and restrained directional flow; route-overlay.js supplies the overview diagram. The presentation is inspired by automotive driving-path visualizations. No Tesla interface, third-party HUD artwork or proprietary navigation code was copied.
The source camera geometry and full main SPZ are retained. The floor offset is estimated, model units are not metres, and there is no surveyed pavement or reliable scan-depth occlusion. This is a recorded-route visualization, not a shortest-path or accessibility service.

EARLIER CONNECTED STUDY
The optional three-scene mode preserves approximate scene transforms and a clearly schematic doorway/lobby/stair connection. The reconstructions have independent coordinate frames and unknown metric scale. Display fades identify unsupported joins. Movement follows captured positions and a narrow proxy corridor; no scanned collision surface is claimed.

DISPLAY AND RESOURCE POLICY
The near-camera projected-splat coverage correction is retained across device-pixel ratios. No source-scan data or route geometry was synthesized to hide missing detail. Fine texture remains limited by the reconstruction.
The public viewer does not wait for local telemetry. Ci explicitly lifted the CPU/GPU limit for the final production session. An optional local guarded mode is retained, implemented with Python standard-library macOS Mach host_statistics and IORegistry IOAccelerator PerformanceStatistics. GPU use is read from Device Utilization %, not inferred from frame rate. No credentials or administrator rights are used. Guarding controls this project, not other applications or all visiting devices.
