From a building
to a place inside.

The campus index locates buildings. A scan adds the experience of being there. Here, three existing Gaussian-splat reconstructions are arranged as one walk: the main outdoor route, its final section, and Avery Hall’s basement.

Where the evidence ends

The scans have independent coordinate systems. Both joins are approximate. The pale lobby and stairway are schematic: the entrance-to-basement connection was not captured or surveyed. This is an exploratory map, not verified wayfinding.

Movement stays near the recorded camera path. The scan is incomplete away from it. Model units are not verified metres; room door positions, travel times and accessible routes are not provided.
